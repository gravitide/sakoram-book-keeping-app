> A handoff document for the agent/designer building the Sakoram marketing
> website. It describes the product, every feature, the pricing tiers, the
> differentiators, and suggested website sections + copy. Screenshots of every

## 1. Product at a glance

| | |
|---|---|
| **Name** | **Sakoram** |
| **Tagline** | *Sakoram — The desktop bookkeeper!* |
| **One-liner** | A fast, fully-offline desktop bookkeeping app for Sri Lankan small businesses — quotes, invoices, bills, payments, payroll, and professional PDFs, all on your own machine. |
| **Platforms** | Windows & macOS desktop app (native, installable). |
| **Category** | Small-business bookkeeping / invoicing / accounting. |
| **Works offline** | 100% — no internet, no account, no cloud required. Your data never leaves your computer. |
| **Audience** | Sri Lankan small businesses, freelancers, accountants, and shops that bill customers, track expenses, and run payroll. |

**Elevator pitch:** Sakoram is bookkeeping software built for Sri Lankan
businesses that want professional invoices and clean books without a monthly
cloud subscription or an internet connection. It runs entirely on your own
computer, keeps every business's data in its own file, prints beautiful PDFs,
and handles the local realities — LKR, VAT, the April–March fiscal year, and
EPF / ETF / PAYE payroll — out of the box.

## 2. Why Sakoram (core value propositions)

Use these as the pillars of the homepage.

1. **Yours, offline, private.** Everything runs locally on your machine. No
   cloud, no sign-up, no subscription-to-access-your-own-data. Your books stay
   on your computer.
2. **Built for Sri Lanka.** LKR by default, VAT reporting, the government
   April–March fiscal year, and full EPF / ETF / PAYE (APIT) payroll — no
   configuration gymnastics.
3. **Professional documents.** Beautiful, print-ready PDF quotes, invoices,
   bills, vouchers, payslips and statements — with your logo, your fonts, your
   brand colour.
4. **Run every business from one app.** Unlimited businesses (on paid tiers),
   each in its own isolated file, switchable in one click.
5. **Fast and focused.** A native desktop app — instant, keyboard-friendly,
   no spinner-laden web pages.
6. **One-time purchase, no rent.** Buy a tier once and own it. A generous free
   tier and a full 30-day trial mean you can start today.

## 3. Feature catalog

Organised by the way the app itself is grouped. Each area notes the screens a
website agent can pair with screenshots.

### 3.1 Dashboard & Calendar
- **Dashboard** — at-a-glance KPI tiles (receivables, payables, cash position,
  paid-this-year), a 12-month cash-flow chart, receivables aging, an
  expenses-by-category donut, top clients, recent activity, an overdue list,
  and quick "New …" actions. Hand-drawn, theme-aware charts.
- **Calendar** — a month-grid view of every upcoming due date in one place:
  invoice due dates, bill due dates, quote expiries, and payslip pay dates,
  with colour-coded kinds, overdue highlighting, and quick-create from any day.

### 3.2 Sales — money coming in
- **Quotes** — create, send, accept/reject, and convert a quote straight into
  an invoice. Bundle or itemised pricing, VAT, optional project title, and a
  one-click professional PDF.
- **Invoices** — issue invoices, record customer payments, and watch the status
  move automatically through unpaid → partial → paid → overdue. Payment ledger
  per invoice.
- **Recurring invoices** — set up invoice templates (weekly / monthly /
  quarterly / yearly) and generate the next batch of draft invoices in one
  click. Pause, edit, or delete templates anytime.
- **Credit notes** — issue a proper negative document for refunds and returns,
  optionally linked to the original invoice.
- **Customer statements** — print a point-in-time PDF statement of everything a
  client still owes, with aging buckets — perfect for chasing payment.

### 3.3 Purchases — money going out
- **Bills** — record vendor bills with categories, track what you owe, and mark
  them paid. Status derives automatically (unpaid / partial / paid / overdue).
- **Recurring bills** — vendor-side templates that generate real bills on a
  cadence (rent, utilities, subscriptions) so nothing slips through.

### 3.4 Money & Banking
- **Vouchers** — a clean receipts (money in) / payments (money out) ledger.
  Every payment against an invoice, bill, or payslip is a voucher, and
  standalone vouchers capture everything else. Printable receipt PDFs.
- **Bank reconciliation** — import your bank statement CSV, auto-match rows to
  your vouchers (by amount, date, and reference), and create vouchers from
  anything unmatched — so your books agree with your bank.

### 3.5 Payroll (Sri-Lanka-ready)
- **Employees** — a payroll address book with NIC, designation, joining date,
  basic salary, and bank-payment details.
- **Payslips** — generate per-employee, per-period payslips with earnings and
  deductions, issue them, record payment, and print a professional payslip PDF.
- **EPF / ETF** — automatic statutory contributions: employee EPF (8%) as a
  live deduction line, employer EPF (12%) + ETF (3%) computed and printed.
  Rates are configurable.
- **PAYE / APIT** — automatic monthly income-tax computation using a
  configurable relief + progressive bracket table (pre-loaded with the Sri
  Lankan 2025/26 table, editable when budgets change).
- **Bulk payroll run** — pick a month, get every active employee, and create,
  issue, and pay the whole run in one flow. Bulk-export every payslip PDF.
- **Payroll dashboard** — upcoming pay-cycle hero with urgency badge, KPIs,
  a month-over-month salary chart, recent runs, and outstanding payroll.

### 3.6 Reports
Printable, date-ranged reports — every one exports to PDF:
- **Profit & Loss** (accrual) — income, less bill expenses and payroll.
- **VAT report** — output VAT vs input VAT and the net payable for the period
  (what you need to file with the IRD).
- **Aged receivables** — who owes you money, bucketed by days overdue.
- **Aged payables** — who you owe, the same way.
- **Cash flow** — receipts in vs payments out, by month (cash basis).
- **Sales by client** & **Expenses by vendor** — drill into where revenue and
  costs come from.
- **Payroll register** — every payslip in a period with earnings, deductions,
  and net.

### 3.7 Contacts & lists
- **Clients** and **Vendors** address books, with archiving, and one-click
  jumps to "all quotes / invoices / bills" for a contact.
- **Bill categories** — a managed, colour-and-icon-coded list for organising
  expenses.

### 3.8 Professional PDFs & branding
- **Studio-quality PDFs** for every document, rendered by a bundled typesetting
  engine (not a browser print) — crisp, consistent, identical on every machine.
- **Your brand** — upload a square identity logo and a wide letterhead logo,
  pick your **brand/accent colour**, and choose from bundled professional
  **fonts** (including monospaced number fonts) for both the app and the PDFs.
- **In-app PDF preview & print** — preview any document and print or save it
  without leaving the app.
- **Attachments** — attach scans/receipts/photos to any document, including a
  slick **phone-upload** flow (scan a QR, snap a photo on your phone, it lands
  on the document over your local network).

### 3.9 Multi-business
- Run **multiple businesses** from one install, each in its own isolated data
  file, switch with one click, each with its own logo, settings, and books.
  (Free tier: up to 2 businesses; paid tiers: unlimited.)

### 3.10 Security & data ownership
- **Per-business encryption (opt-in)** — lock a business behind a password;
  its database is encrypted at rest with a recovery key for safety. A quick
  "lock" button is always at hand.
- **Password-protected PDFs (opt-in)** — apply an owner password to generated
  PDFs to prevent editing/copying.
- **Backups you own** — export any business (with its attachments and logo) to
  a single file, and import it on another machine — optionally passphrase-
  encrypted. Your data is always portable and never held hostage.

### 3.11 Personalisation
- **Light / dark / system** theme with a warm "paper" light mode.
- **Brand accent colour** (8-swatch palette) flows through the whole app and
  your PDFs.
- **Independent UI and PDF fonts** from a bundled library.
- **Interface zoom** (80–150%) for comfortable reading.
- Custom currencies if you operate outside the built-in set (LKR, USD, EUR,
  GBP, INR, AED, AUD, SGD).

### 3.12 In-app help
- A built-in **help library** of plain-English bookkeeping explainers tuned for
  Sri Lankan businesses (what a credit note is, how VAT works, fiscal year,
  EPF/ETF/PAYE), opened in its own window — so users never feel lost.

## 4. Pricing & tiers

Sakoram is sold as a **one-time purchase per tier** (perpetual licence — buy
once, own it; no subscription). Every install starts with a **30-day full-
featured (Premium) free trial**, then drops to a **free Basic tier** forever.
A licence key unlocks Plus or Premium permanently.

- **Basic — "Invoice & get paid, free."** Everything a tiny business needs to

## 5. Differentiators (what to lead with vs. competitors)

- **Genuinely offline & private.** Most modern bookkeeping tools are cloud SaaS.
  Sakoram is a desktop app that needs no internet and stores nothing online —
  a real selling point for privacy-conscious owners and unreliable-connectivity
  contexts.
- **One-time purchase, not a subscription.** No monthly rent to see your own
  data.
- **Sri-Lanka-native.** LKR, VAT/IRD, April–March fiscal year, and EPF/ETF/PAYE
  baked in — not bolted on.
- **Professional, consistent PDFs.** Typeset output, not browser printouts.
- **Multi-business from one app**, each isolated and individually encryptable.
- **You own your data** — file-level backups, export/import, nothing locked in.

## 9. FAQ seeds

- **Does it need the internet?** No — Sakoram is fully offline. Nothing is sent
  to any server; your data lives on your computer.
- **Is it a subscription?** No — it's a one-time purchase per tier. There's a
  free Basic tier and a 30-day full trial.
- **Can I run more than one business?** Yes — unlimited on Plus/Premium (up to
  2 on free Basic), each isolated in its own file.
- **Does it handle Sri Lankan payroll?** Yes — EPF, ETF, and PAYE/APIT with
  configurable rates and the current tax table (Premium).
- **Will it do VAT?** Yes — a VAT report of output vs input VAT and net payable
  for the period.
- **Is my data safe?** It stays on your machine. You can password-encrypt any
  business, password-protect PDFs, and export full backups you control.
- **What platforms?** Windows and macOS.
- **Can I move my data to another computer?** Yes — export a business to a
  single file (optionally encrypted) and import it anywhere.
